CHĀṆAKYA & MAHABHARATA DIPLOMACY

The Four Upāyas of Campus Harmony

Ancient strategic treatises outline four progressive methods (चतुरूपाय) to resolve discord. Learn when and how to deploy each:

Stage 1 साम

Sāma (Conciliation)

Empathetic listening, cordial speech, acknowledging positive intent, and finding common goals.

Campus Application: "Let's grab a coffee and hear everyone's side before locking any decisions. We all want an 'A' grade."
Stage 2 दान

Dāna (Fair Concession)

Strategic trade-offs, win-win workload rebalancing, and offering incentives or credit sharing.

Campus Application: "If you handle the frontend layout and documentation, I will write the backend endpoints and slides."
Stage 3 भेद

Bheda (Perspective Shift)

Differentiating personalities, separating facts from wounded egos, and deconstructing complex deadlock into distinct independent parts.

Campus Application: "We aren't arguing about who is smarter; we are simply deciding whether feature X fits within a 3-day buffer."
Stage 4 दण्ड

Daṇḍa (Firm Accountability)

Setting non-negotiable boundaries, invoking clear rubric rules, and escalating to institutional authority (Professor/TA) as a transparent last resort.

Campus Application: "If we don't have your section by Friday 6 PM, we will submit the remaining sections and inform Prof. Sharma."

How to Deploy "Kurukshetra to Campus" Online

This web app is pure HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript—ready for zero-setup 100% free hosting in under 60 seconds!

⭐ Recommended (No Git Required)

Method 1: Netlify Drop

⏱️ 30 Seconds • Free Forever
  1. Open app.netlify.com/drop in your browser.
  2. Drag & drop the entire kurukshetra-to-campus folder right into the browser window.
  3. Boom! Netlify gives you an instant live HTTPS link (e.g., https://kurukshetra-to-campus.netlify.app).
  4. You can customize the domain name under Site Settings > Change site name!

Method 2: Vercel

⏱️ 1 Minute • Blazing Fast
  1. Go to vercel.com/new.
  2. Connect your GitHub account or upload your project folder.
  3. Set Framework Preset to "Other" and click Deploy.
  4. Your site goes live with automated global CDN and HTTPS!

Method 3: GitHub Pages

⏱️ 2 Minutes • Great for Portfolios
  1. Create a new public repository on GitHub (e.g. kurukshetra-to-campus).
  2. Upload index.html, style.css, app.js, hero-banner.jpg, and logo.jpg.
  3. Go to Settings > Pages.
  4. Under Branch, select main and folder / (root), then click Save.
  5. Live at https://your-username.github.io/kurukshetra-to-campus!

Method 4: Run Locally (Test Right Now)

⏱️ Instant

Run this one-liner in your terminal / PowerShell:

python -m http.server 8000

Then open http://localhost:8000 in your browser!
